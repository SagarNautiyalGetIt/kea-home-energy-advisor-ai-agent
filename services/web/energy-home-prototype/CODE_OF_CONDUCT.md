# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work rather than attacking the person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this standard and may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately through the parent repository's project channels rather than posting sensitive details publicly.

## Attribution

This concise policy is informed by the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
